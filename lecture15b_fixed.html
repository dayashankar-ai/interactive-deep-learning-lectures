• Perfect for classification final layers

Key Insights

  • Max Pooling: Preserves strongest features (edges, corners)
  • Average Pooling: Smooths features, reduces noise
  • Global Pooling: Ultimate compression for final layers
  • Size Selection: Larger pools = more compression, less detail
