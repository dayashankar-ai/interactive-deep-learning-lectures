🔍 Convolution: The Detective's Magnifying Glass

🔎 Detective Alex's Secret Investigation Tool

Detective Alex has a magical magnifying glass called Convolution! This isn't an ordinary magnifying glass - it can slide across any image and detect specific clues like edges, corners, or patterns! 🕵️‍♀️

🔍➡️📸➡️🔍➡️✨

The amazing thing is that this magnifying glass can be programmed to look for different types of clues. One setting finds vertical lines, another finds horizontal lines, and another finds curves!

🔢 The Convolution Operation: Mathematical Magic

Output[i,j] = Σ Σ (Input[i+m,j+n] × Filter[m,n])
Output[i,j]
= The clue Detective Alex found at position (i,j)
Input[i+m,j+n]
= Pixel values in the image under the magnifying glass
Filter[m,n]
= The detective's magnifying glass settings
Σ Σ
= Add up all the multiplications (double summation)
