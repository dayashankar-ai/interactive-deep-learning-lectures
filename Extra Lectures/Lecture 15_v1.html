1980s - 1990s
🧠 The Foundation Era

The Dream Begins: Scientists first imagined teaching computers to see by mimicking how animal brains process vision. The concept of convolution (sliding window patterns) was born!

Professor Alex says: "It's like teaching someone to read by showing them letters, then words, then sentences - building up from simple to complex!"

1998
👶 LeNet - The First Baby CNN

The Breakthrough: Yann LeCun created LeNet-5, the first successful CNN that could read handwritten digits for postal services and banks!

What made it special: It had just 7 layers but proved that the convolution concept actually worked in real life!

Fun fact: LeNet processed images smaller than your phone's camera icon today, but it was revolutionary!

2012
⚡ AlexNet - The Game Changer

The Revolution: Alex Krizhevsky's AlexNet shocked the world by winning the ImageNet competition by a massive margin!

Secret weapons:
• Used powerful GPUs for the first time
• Introduced ReLU activation (like giving neurons more energy!)
• Used dropout (randomly turning off neurons to prevent laziness)

Impact: This victory started the Deep Learning boom that changed the world!

2014
🏗️ VGG - The Architect's Masterpiece

The Philosophy: Oxford's VGG team believed in "go deeper" - they showed that using many small filters worked better than few large ones!

Innovation: Used 3x3 filters consistently throughout, proving that simplicity and depth could create powerful architectures.

Legacy: VGG's clear, systematic design inspired countless future architectures!

🏛️ Meet the Famous Architecture Family

Professor Alex's Architecture Family

"Think of CNN architectures like a family of brilliant inventors, each with their own personality and special talents! Let me introduce you to the most famous members of this family, and you'll see how each one contributed something unique to the world of computer vision."

👶
LeNet-5 (1998)
"The Pioneer Baby"
• First successful CNN
• 7 layers total
• Read handwritten digits
• Used sigmoid activation
• Proved CNNs could work!
⚡
AlexNet (2012)
"The Revolutionary Thunder"
• 8 layers deep
• First to use ReLU
• GPU-powered training
• Dropout for learning
• Won ImageNet by huge margin!
🏗️
VGG (2014)
"The Systematic Architect"
• 16-19 layers deep
• All 3x3 filters
• Simple, elegant design
• Showed "deeper is better"
• Clear architectural pattern
